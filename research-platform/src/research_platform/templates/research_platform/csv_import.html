The columns

Download a blank CSV with every column as its header row, and fill it in. In Excel, save it as CSV UTF-8 (Comma delimited): the file must be UTF-8, with its columns separated by commas. The first row names the columns, in any order.

Every row needs:

  • subject: the subject's label, as you'll know them here.
  • time: the birth time as the source gives it, a date and time without an offset, such as 1987-06-15T04:25 or 1987-06-15 04:25.
  • time_kind: what time is. utc: a UTC time. zone: the clock in zone. offset: the clock at utc_offset. local-mean-time: the birthplace's local mean time.
  • latitude and longitude: the birthplace in decimal degrees, north and east positive.

And may have:

  • zone: for a zone row, a time zone by its name in the time-zone database, such as Europe/London.
  • utc_offset: for an offset row, its offset from UTC, such as +01:00, -05:30 or +00:09:21. A zone row may state it too, and it's checked against the zone.
  • elevation_m: the birthplace's elevation, in metres.
  • time_resolution: how precisely the time was recorded: hour, minute or second.
  • stated_uncertainty_s: the uncertainty the source states, in seconds.
  • accept_pre_1970_tzdb: yes to accept the time-zone database's offset for a zone row before 1970, where it's less certain.
